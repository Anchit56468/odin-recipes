Pan Bagnat

Ingredients

  • 1 round loaf of crusty bread
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs, sliced
  • 1/2 cup canned tuna, drained
  • 1/4 cup black olives, pitted and sliced
  • 1/4 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/4 cup cucumber, sliced
  • 1/4 cup red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon red wine vinegar
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Slice the round loaf of bread in half horizontally.
  2. In a bowl, combine the sliced eggs, tuna, olives, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, and red onion.
  3. Drizzle the olive oil and red wine vinegar over the mixture and toss to combine. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  4. Spread the mixture evenly over the bottom half of the bread.
  5. Place the top half of the bread on top of the filling and press down gently.
  6. Wrap the sandwich tightly in plastic wrap and let it sit for at least 30 minutes to allow the flavors to meld together.
  7. Slice the sandwich into wedges and serve.
